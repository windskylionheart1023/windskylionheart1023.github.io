Teaching assistant for the master's course *Stochastic Signal and System Analysis* ([H05I7A](https://onderwijsaanbod.kuleuven.be/syllabi/e/H05I7AE.htm)), taught by Prof. Hugo Van hamme and Prof. Patrick Wambacq. I give the exercise sessions.
